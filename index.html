06 / PROFESSIONAL DIRECTION

Explore career pathways

Five connected directions where biotechnology, computation and data science can contribute to life-science research.

01
⌬

Bioinformatics

Organizing, comparing and interpreting DNA, RNA and protein information with computational methods.

Relevant toolkit

BLAST, Biopython, Bioconductor and UCSC Genome Browser.

Potential roles

Bioinformatics Analyst · Genomics Data Analyst · Bioinformatics Researcher

Learning direction

Sequence alignment, biological databases, genome annotation and reproducible analysis. Future project ideas include sequence-analysis exercises and public genomic-data reports.

02
⌘

Computational Biology

Applying mathematical, statistical and computational approaches to understand biological systems.

Relevant toolkit

Python, R, statistics, biological databases and computational analysis.

Potential roles

Computational Biologist · Research Associate · Biological Data Scientist

Learning direction

Biological modelling, simulation, genomics and protein analysis. A strong foundation in biology, mathematics, programming and statistics will support this direction.

03
◎

Genomics & Precision Medicine

Studying genomes, genetic variation and gene expression to understand biology and support personalized-health research.

Relevant toolkit

Genome browsers, sequence-analysis tools, statistics and visualization.

Potential roles

Genomics Analyst · Genomics Research Associate · Genomic Data Scientist

Learning direction

Genome organization, genetic variation and gene-expression data. Clinical use requires appropriate training, validation and regulatory oversight.

04
✳

AI & Machine Learning in Biology

Using carefully evaluated data-driven models to explore patterns and research questions in life sciences.

Relevant toolkit

Python, scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas and visualization libraries.

Potential roles

Biological Data Scientist · ML Research Associate · AI-in-Healthcare Researcher

Learning direction

Statistics, data quality, biological context and model evaluation. A prediction is not automatically scientific or clinical evidence.

05
⌖

Computational Drug Discovery

Exploring computational ways to study molecules, proteins and potential drug candidates.

Relevant toolkit

PyMOL, protein-modelling concepts, computational analysis and biological data.

Potential roles

Drug Discovery Researcher · Molecular Modelling Associate · Pharmaceutical Research Associate

Learning direction

Protein structures, protein–ligand interactions and virtual screening. Computational methods support research prioritization; they do not replace experiments or establish safety and effectiveness.

Bioinformatics emphasizes biological data; computational biology studies systems and models; genomics explores genome-scale questions; AI/ML develops predictive methods; computational drug discovery supports early-stage research.
